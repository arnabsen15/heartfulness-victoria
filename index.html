About

Heartfulness is a simple approach to meditation that many people practise in community. Local volunteers and friends across Victoria host free or low-barrier group sessions so anyone can sit together in a calm, welcoming environment.

Community sessions

Gatherings are organised by local people in libraries, neighbourhood houses, and community centres — ordinary places made a little quieter for an hour or so.

No experience needed

You do not need prior practice. Sessions are usually guided gently and open to beginners as well as regular meditators.

What to expect

A few practical notes so your first (or next) visit feels easy.

  • 1

    Arrive a little early

    Give yourself time to find the room, settle in, and switch phones to silent before the session begins.

  • 2

    Sit comfortably

    Chairs are usually available. Wear clothes you can sit in easily — no special gear required.

  • 3

    Free unless noted

    Most community sessions are free. If a venue or event asks for a donation or fee, that will be noted on the event listing.

  • 4

    Beginners welcome

    You are welcome whether meditation is new to you or part of your routine. Come as you are.
