About

Heartfulness is a simple approach to meditation practised in community. Local volunteers across Victoria host free or low-barrier group sessions so anyone can sit together in a calm, welcoming space.

Community sessions

Libraries, neighbourhood houses, and community centres — ordinary places made quieter for an hour.

No experience needed

Sessions are gently guided and open to beginners as well as regular meditators.

Across Victoria

From Melton to Berwick, Knox to Kalkallo — check the events list for a venue near you.

What to expect

A few practical notes so your visit feels easy.

  • 1

    Arrive a little early

    Find the room, settle in, and switch phones to silent.

  • 2

    Sit comfortably

    Chairs are usually available. No special gear required.

  • 3

    Free unless noted

    Most sessions are free. Any fee or donation is listed on the event.

  • 4

    Beginners welcome

    Come as you are — whether meditation is new or familiar.
